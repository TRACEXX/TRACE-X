CASE IMAGES
===========
Photographs that are EVIDENCE (scene photos, screenshots, stills) should not
go here. Upload them from the case's Evidence Vault tab so they are hashed,
stored privately in evidence_storage/ and tracked in the chain of custody.

The sample "photos" in the seed data are generated placeholder images. To
replace them, upload your own staged photo as new evidence (registered
evidence is never overwritten, by design).
