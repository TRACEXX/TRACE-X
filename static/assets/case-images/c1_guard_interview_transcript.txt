INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT (FICTIONAL TRAINING RECORD)
Case: CASE-001   Interviewee: Ghufran Siddique, night guard   Interviewer: Det. Warisha Ahmed
Date: 14 March 2026, 09:40

WA: Tell me about your shift on the 13th.
GS: I started at 20:00. Professor Rafiq came in around ten past eight. He often worked late on Thursdays.
WA: Did you hear anything unusual?
GS: Around five to eleven I heard raised voices from the second floor. Two men. One shouted "that ledger is not yours to sell".
    It stopped after about a minute.
WA: Did you go up?
GS: Not then. On my 23:00 round the Rare Books Room door was closed and the light was on. I thought he was still working.
WA: Did you see anyone leave?
GS: No. But at about twenty past eleven I heard the side door bang. The side entrance is not visible from my desk.
WA: Who has cards for the side door?
GS: Only Wahid sahib and the professor. Wahid sahib told me last month he "lost" his card once, then found it again.
WA: When did you open the Rare Books Room?
GS: At ten past seven in the morning, at the end of my shift. That is when I found him.
