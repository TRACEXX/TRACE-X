WAREHOUSE INCIDENT REPORT 
Case reference : CASE-004 "The Indigo Veil"          Date of report: 14 March 2026
Location       : Warehouse 03, West Wharf District, Karachi

At 02:10 on 13 March a patrol responded to a call from the warehouse night watchman (Karim Bux) about "a van and
two people with bags" at the rear gate. On arrival the van had left. Recovered from the loading bay:
  1. Eleven sealed packets, each stamped with an indigo crescent (approx. 1.9 kg total) - sent to lab (see CASE-004 lab report)
  2. A handwritten ledger with dates, initials and quantities (photographed - see ledger page)
  3. A black prepaid mobile phone, battery 31%, unlocked by forensic tools on 15 March (see chat extract)
  4. A torn equipment label: "NEON WHARF LOUNGE - AUDIO - CASE 4 OF 6"
CCTV: rear-gate camera CAM-3 shows an exchange of two duffel bags at 01:14 (see still).
Hospital link: at 03:40 Civil Hospital admitted a 22-year-old man with an overdose; an indigo-crescent packet was found
in his pocket. He had been at an after-party at Neon Wharf Lounge.
